## Hotel cleaning in Phuket — the reinforcement you can call on when the rooms are full

Hotels in Phuket know well that in the high season the permanent housekeeping team is already at full stretch and still not enough. Rooms have to turn over in time for check-in, common areas take many times the usual wear, while hiring extra permanent staff to cover just a few months is a cost that does not pay off across the rest of the year. Phuket Maid Service's **hotel cleaning in Phuket** is the B2B answer: adding trained maid teams to work alongside the hotel's own staff only when needed, whether daily, monthly or for the whole season. Every member of staff is police background-checked — a requirement hotel HR departments already have — so work can start without any extra screening step.

## Common areas are a guest's first impression

The lobby, the large glass doors, the stair rails and the corridor floors are the first thing guests see before reaching their room, and the backdrop of the photos guests take most. This work needs both a frequency and a technique different from cleaning rooms. Our team looks after the building glass within reach using professional squeegee equipment, wipes lobby furniture, keeps common bathrooms in order through the day, and hoses down the entrance floor so it is ready for guests throughout opening hours. For hotels with marble floors that have started to dull under thousands of shoes and luggage wheels a month, we offer [marble floor polishing](/en/services/marble-polishing-phuket/) at ฿250/sqm to bring the shine back to the lobby, scheduled during quieter periods so it never affects the guest-facing image.
