## Resort cleaning in Phuket — many buildings, many zones, planned differently from a hotel

A high-rise hotel gathers everything into one tower, but a resort spreads its rooms across several buildings or dozens of villa units over a wide site, where walking from the lobby to the farthest unit can take several minutes. Cleaning is therefore not just a matter of headcount, but of planning the route and the order of zones so the team moves efficiently without doubling back all day. Phuket Maid Service's **resort cleaning in Phuket** starts by walking the resort layout with your manager, splitting the site into zones, deciding how many people cover each point, then entering by round on the agreed schedule. Every member of the team is trained and police background-checked, with ฿100,000 damage insurance covering every job.

## Outdoor work is the heart of a resort — salas, garden paths and the floors around the buildings

What most clearly sets a resort apart from a hotel is the proportion of outdoor space. Garden-side salas, the walkways linking the buildings, benches and sun loungers take sun and rain in turn all year, building up algae, matted leaves and cobwebs along the eaves and wooden roof structures. Guests always pass these spots before reaching their room, so their feeling about the resort is judged from the walkway onward. Our team takes on hosing down walkways and common floors, clearing cobwebs from sala roof structures with extension tools, and wiping outdoor furniture ready for use. For sand-wash walkways gone dull or slippery from deep-set algae, use the [sand-wash scrub and wax service](/en/services/sandwash-polishing-phuket/) at ฿100/sqm to restore the whole path, on a budget you can work out in advance from the actual area.

## The low season is the prime window for the big clean

The rainy season, when occupancy eases, is the time a resort should invest in its annual deep clean — because you can close one building at a time for renovation without guests in the others feeling it at all.
