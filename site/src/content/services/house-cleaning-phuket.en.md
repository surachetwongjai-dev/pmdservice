## Dry-season dust, rainy-season mould — the challenges unique to Phuket homes

Homes in Phuket face two sharply contrasting seasons more than most provinces. In the dry season, the wind carries dust from the roads and nearby construction onto window frames, insect screens and the backs of cabinets within just a few days. In the rainy season, which lasts much of the year, built-up humidity brings musty smells to closed rooms and mould along bathroom grout, on rain-facing walls and in the nooks under the sink. Our team therefore orders the work by season: in the dry months we focus on clearing dust from hidden spots such as the tops of tall cabinets, ceiling fans and window tracks; in the rains we focus on scrubbing grout, disinfecting damp points and airing out rooms that are rarely used, so the house stays dry, clean and free of the musty smell that would otherwise greet you at the door.
