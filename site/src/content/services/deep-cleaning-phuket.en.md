## Deep Cleaning in Phuket — when a home needs more than a routine tidy

A home shut up for months, a condo just handed over, a house after renovation with cement dust on every surface, or a home lived in so long that build-up outpaces the weekly clean — these all call for **Deep Cleaning**, or **Big Cleaning**: a big team clean that works through every corner systematically, not just what the eye can see.

The **deep cleaning team** at Phuket Maid Service works to an agreed checklist, from ceiling to floor: remove cobwebs and dust on tall cabinets, wipe ceiling fans and lights, scrub limescale and soap scum across the whole bathroom, strip kitchen grease, clean door and window tracks and fly screens, and vacuum, mop and sanitise the floor under every movable piece of furniture. When it is done, we review it with you zone by zone.

## Who it is for

Our Deep Cleaning customers fall into three main groups. First, **homes before move-in / after handover** who want to start in a genuinely clean home from day one, especially clearing invisible construction dust you would otherwise breathe every day. Second, **homes after renovation or construction**, where cement dust needs repeated wet-wiping technique, not just sweeping and mopping. Third, **homes or villas left vacant for a while** — holiday homes and rentals that need to be reopened with confidence.

For pool-villa owners and Airbnb hosts, a Big Cleaning on a cycle (say every 3–6 months) alongside regular between-guest maids keeps cleanliness reviews high — and booked together with [steam sofa & mattress cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/) in one visit, we make the package better value than booking separately.
