## Post-renovation cleaning in Phuket — the cement dust no amount of sweeping clears

Anyone who has renovated a house or condo knows that once the builders pack up and leave, the job isn''t over — because what they leave behind is **fine cement dust** clinging to every surface: on cabinets, inside drawers, on skirting, in window tracks and floating in the air. Sweep or vacuum, and the dust lifts straight back up and re-settles. This is exactly why **post-renovation cleaning in Phuket** needs a different technique from ordinary cleaning — our team uses **repeated wet-wiping** to genuinely capture the dust, not just move it from one place to another.

Beyond cement dust, renovation work usually leaves **builders'' specific marks** too: paint drips on the floor, glue residue from laying materials, and silicone grout smeared on glass and around sanitaryware. The team scrapes and wipes these off with the tools and solutions suited to each surface, taking care not to scratch the floor or glass.

## Why it must be done before your furniture moves back

Timing is critical for this job. It should be done **after the builders fully finish and before your furniture moves back**, because once the sofa, mattress and cabinets return to a room that still has cement dust floating around, the dust works into the fabric and the gaps in furniture — becoming a long-term problem many times harder to remove. Cleaning an empty room lets the team reach every corner thoroughly, so when the furniture comes in afterwards, the home is genuinely clean and ready to live in.

If your home has just had new flooring laid too, many customers pair this job with floor polishing in one visit for better value — the team can assess it for you.
