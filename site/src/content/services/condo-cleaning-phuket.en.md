## Condo cleaning in Phuket — the unit is small, but the details are not

A one-bedroom condo looks like a small job, but anyone who has done it themselves knows the real time goes into the details: water marks on the shower screen that come back no matter how much you wipe, dust on top of built-in wardrobes you have to climb to reach, the corners of the balcony where pigeons visit, and the large view windows that are the easiest thing in the unit to smear. Phuket Maid Service's **condo cleaning in Phuket** is designed to finish fast without skipping any of these. The rate is ฿400 per hour (excl. tax), and most studios to one-bedroom units are done in just a few hours — ideal both for residents who want their day off back and for owners preparing to hand over to a new tenant.

## Building rules — let the team coordinate them for you

What sets condo work apart from a house is the building rules. Some buildings require management to be notified before an outside party is brought in, some require a visitor pass to be exchanged at the lobby every time, and many restrict the hours for the service lift when carrying cleaning equipment. Our team works in condo buildings all across Phuket and knows these steps well. Just tell us the building name when you book, and we prepare the documents, send the names of our police background-checked staff to management, and arrange the entry and exit times in full. The owner does not need to take a day off to open the door or chase management themselves.
