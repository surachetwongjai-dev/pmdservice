## Move-out cleaning in Phuket — return the room that passes inspection, get the full deposit back

When you moved in, you put down one or two months'' deposit, and when you move out that money should come back in full. But the reality is that many tenants lose part of their deposit — and the number-one reason isn''t damage, it''s **cleanliness**. A bathroom with limescale, a kitchen with grease baked onto the stove wall, cabinets that weren''t wiped, or marks on the walls are all excuses to deduct. **Move-out cleaning in Phuket** helps you return the room as close as possible to how you received it, to reduce the reasons for a deposit deduction.

Our advantage is that we **know where inspectors look**. From experience with returning rentals and condos in Phuket, the team knows owners and agents tend to check the same spots — the bathroom first, then the kitchen, inside cabinets and the walls. So we focus on these spots in particular, rather than just cleaning the whole room in passing.

## Do it while the room is empty, before inspection day

The best moment is **after everything is moved out**, because an empty room lets us clean thoroughly — including behind cabinets and corners that were hidden by furniture, which are often exactly where dust and marks build up unnoticed. Then schedule it to finish before the day the owner or agent inspects the room, leaving a little time in case there are spots they want touched up. Most clients here are condo tenants in tourist areas like [Patong](/en/locations/patong/) and city workers relocating.
