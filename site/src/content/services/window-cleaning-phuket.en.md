## Seaside glass — why the marks come back every time you wipe it yourself

Sea-view homes and condos in Phuket face three enemies of glass at once. First is **salt haze** off the sea breeze, building up into a thin cloudy film across the whole panel. Second is **water marks** from rain and sprinkler spray that dry on the surface until the minerals set as white spots. Third is **road dust** that binds with humidity into a sticky grime. Grab a cloth and ordinary glass cleaner and you usually end up with streaky swirls, because you''re only smearing the grime around, not actually dissolving it off the surface.

Our team uses the same process you see in the video above: apply the dissolving solution all over, scrub gently with gear that won''t scratch the glass, then **squeegee the water off** in continuous strokes from top to bottom. The glass dries clear straight away, with no water streaks or cloth lint — unlike amateur wiping that only shows more streaks when the sun hits it.

## Tall, large panels — don''t risk climbing yourself

Villas and modern homes in Phuket love big floor-to-ceiling glass to open up the view. The prettier it is, the harder it is to clean, because the top half is out of arm''s reach. Climbing a ladder to do it yourself isn''t worth one slip. Our team uses **long extension poles with washing and squeegee heads**, working from the ground up to mezzanine glass, transoms above doors and tall panels beside staircases. The result is even across the whole panel, top edge to bottom, and far safer than climbing. Every job is covered by ฿100,000 damage insurance.
