## Villa cleaning in Phuket — a large home needs a team, not a single maid

A three to five-bedroom villa has many times the space and detail of an ordinary house: a separate bathroom in every bedroom, high open ceilings where dust and cobwebs settle beyond the reach of an ordinary duster, large sliding glass doors all around the house, and lounge areas both indoors and out. Sending in a single maid is a recipe for a job that never ends. Phuket Maid Service's **villa cleaning in Phuket** therefore always starts by assessing the property, so we can size the team to the house, split the zones between them, and work systematically from the top floor down to finish every room in a single visit. The rate is charged by the actual hours at ฿400 per hour, with a clear total quoted before every job.

## Valuable decor — trust needs evidence behind it

Many villa owners invest in imported furniture, artwork and decor that is hard to put a number on. The first question before letting anyone into the house is therefore not about price, but about the safety of their property. We answer with standards you can actually verify: every maid is trained and police background-checked before starting, every job is covered by ฿100,000 damage insurance, and the team is trained to choose products by surface, never using abrasives on solid wood, natural stone or metal finishes. For villas with marble floors that have started to dull and scratch from use, we offer [marble floor polishing](/en/services/marble-polishing-phuket/) at ฿250/sqm, which can be added into the same visit.

## Owners living abroad can leave the whole house in our care

Plenty of villas in Rawai, Kamala and Mai Khao are owned by foreigners who stay only part of the year. A house closed up for months in Phuket's humid air needs someone to come in, air it out, dust it and check its condition periodically — otherwise the day the owner returns they find musty smells, mould on the walls and furniture that has aged faster than it should. We take on regular weekly or monthly care (flat-rate pricing on request), with a photo report after every visit, and before the owner flies back we run a Big Cleaning from ฿4,000, cleaning the whole house thoroughly so it is ready to live in from day one. For villas with a pool that are let out daily, see our [pool villa cleaning service](/en/services/pool-villa-cleaning-phuket/), scheduled specifically around the guest calendar.
