## Monthly maid in Phuket — consistency you can rely on all month

Hiring a personal maid to work at your home sounds like good value — right up until the day she is off sick, heads back to her home province, or resigns without notice, and suddenly there is no one to look after the home or office. A **monthly maid in Phuket** from Phuket Maid Service fixes this weak point with a team system: you get a regular maid who knows your home, but when she is on leave we have a **stand-in maid to step in straight away** with your home''s checklist, so the work keeps to the same schedule and you never have to go hunting for someone new yourself.

A monthly package is a flat rate by the days and hours per month, so you get a better per-hour rate than booking one-off, and you can budget with certainty. It suits three main groups: homes that want continuity, rental villas that must stay guest-ready at all times, and offices that need a maid every working day.

## Full paperwork, ready for your accounts, right for a legal entity

What corporate customers value most is the paperwork. We operate under **Phu Anda Co., Ltd.**, so we can issue quotations and invoices under a company name — right for offices, clinics, shops and building management entities that need paperwork for their accounts. The scope of work is set out as a clear checklist from the start of the contract, and a team leader inspects the quality of work on a cycle, so the standard holds steady for the life of the contract — not just good for the first month before it slips.

Security is to the same standard as every service: every maid — regular and stand-in alike — is trained and **police background-checked**, and comes with **฿100,000 damage insurance**, so you can leave a home or office with valuables in their care with complete peace of mind.
