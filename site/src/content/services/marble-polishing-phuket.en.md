## Marble polishing in Phuket — restore the shine to your most expensive surface

A marble floor is the face of a property, but in Phuket's seaside climate — sand tracked in on shoes, humidity and salt air — the surface dulls, hairline scratches appear, and water marks form faster than elsewhere. This is especially true for pool villas, where guests walk in wet from the pool and the poolside and living room can visibly fade within a few months. **Marble polishing** with the right machine and compound restores the shine without re-laying the floor, which costs many times more.

## A free trial patch — the policy B2B clients love most

Floor work is something you "have to see with your own eyes", so we let you **trial a small patch on the real floor before deciding** — especially for large areas such as a hotel lobby, resort walkway or a whole pool villa. A building manager or juristic person can have the team polish a demo patch to show the difference between treated and untreated zones, then approve the budget. It is transparent, verifiable and makes the case to management far easier.

For **sandwash** floors around pools, car parks and walkways, we use high-pressure washing to remove moss and deep grime, followed by a **wax coating** that slows new build-up. The floor dries fast and is usable the same day.
