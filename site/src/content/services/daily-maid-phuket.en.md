## Daily maid in Phuket — clear a whole month of housework in a single day

Some days the housework does not just need a couple of hours of help — it needs someone on it all day to finish it in one go. Your parents are flying in to stay this weekend, the home has just been through a birthday party with glasses and plates piled in the sink, or you are back from a long trip to dust, a mountain of laundry and a kitchen you left mid-way before you travelled. The **daily maid in Phuket** service from Phuket Maid Service is built for exactly these days. One maid (or more) settles into your home from the morning and works through the list until it is all done before the day is out.

The rate is straightforward at **฿400/hour, billed by the actual hours worked** (excl. tax) — not a flat day rate where you have to guess whether it is worth it. Work seven hours and you pay for seven; if the job finishes faster than estimated, you pay only for the hours actually used. Unlike an [hourly maid](/en/services/maid-hourly-phuket/), which suits short 2–4 hour jobs, daily hire is the right fit when the **volume** of work is the real problem and you want everything finished within a single day without booking several visits.

## A full day — what a maid can get done

Before starting, the team walks the list with you and orders the jobs to fit the time available. A real day plan from our customers: start the morning with the heaviest zones — the kitchen and every bathroom — then vacuum and mop the whole home through the late morning, tackle bedrooms in the afternoon, change the linen and set up the guest room, and finish by tidying whatever is left. By evening the whole home is ready to use again, as if someone had pressed a reset button.

Regular customers of this service fall into three clear groups: homeowners expecting relatives or guests who want the home ready by tomorrow; homes after a party that need clearing both inside and out; and families who have let the housework build up for weeks and decide to clear it all at once. The team speaks both Thai and English, so foreign homeowners can brief the work themselves with no interpreter needed.
