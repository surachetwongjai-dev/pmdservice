## Airbnb cleaning in Phuket — your cleanliness score is your income

In the short-term rental business, the review that carries the most weight in the next guest's decision is "cleanliness". Guests will forgive a view that does not match the photos, but marked linens, a bathroom with stains, or a musty smell in the room usually end in a one-star review that haunts your bookings for months. Our **Airbnb cleaning in Phuket** is designed specifically for turnovers: cleaning and setting the room ready for new guests in the short window between the old guests checking out and the new ones checking in, finished within the same day.

The heart of this work is certainty of timing. A property taking guests back to back cannot miss a single turnover, so the team works from the guest schedule the host sends in advance, always timing the visit to start after the old guests leave and finish before the new ones arrive. For hosts with a property in a fast-turnover area like [Nai Yang](/en/locations/naiyang/), where guests come and go by flight times, locking in a regular slot guarantees a team is free every round.

## Checklist plus photo reports, so the host can manage from anywhere

Many hosts are not in Phuket — some manage their property from Bangkok or abroad — so we work on a system that is transparent and verifiable from a distance. Each unit has its own standing checklist, from the linen set used and the supplies to restock through to the points that need special attention. Once the room is set, the maid **photographs every room and sends the report on LINE**, so the host can see at once that it is ready for guests, and if anything is damaged or missing, the team reports it with photos immediately, so the host can decide on the deposit or notify the guest in time.

Trust matters just as much, because the maid has to come and go from a property holding the host's and guests' belongings. Every maid is therefore trained and **police background-checked**, with **฿100,000 damage insurance** covering every visit.

## Raise the property on a schedule, not just turnovers

Beyond regular turnovers, a property should have a larger clean on a schedule — steam-cleaning the mattress and sofa to clear dust mites, scrubbing built-up bathroom grime, and reaching the spots a normal turnover does not go deep into. Many hosts pair the turnover service with [steam sofa and mattress cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/) and a Big Cleaning every one to three months, so the property stays in review-photo condition all the time.
