## Curtain cleaning with no removal — protecting both the fabric and the rail

The classic problem with sending curtains off to a shop is what happens after you get them back: the fabric shrinks until the hems hang unevenly, the colours fade from being washed with other fabrics, a few hooks go missing, and the rail is loose or twisted from being taken down and refitted. Made-to-measure curtains that were cut to fit the window suffer most, because even a little shrinkage shows immediately. Phuket Maid Service''s **curtain cleaning in Phuket** cuts out all of this by cleaning the whole curtain **while it still hangs on its rail** — not a single hook removed. No bare windows waiting days for curtains to come back, and no soaking, so the fabric doesn''t shrink.

The heart of the method is the heat of steam, which lifts dust and sanitises within the fabric without any twisting or spinning — the same system as our [sofa cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/), simply adjusted to suit vertically hung fabric. Done set by set, a whole home can be finished in a single day.

## Dust and mould spots — courtesy of air-con and Phuket humidity

Curtains are the front line taking both dust from the window and cold air-con air all day long. The spots where the air-con blows past, combined with Phuket''s high humidity, turn into damp patches, then slowly appear as tiny **black mould spots** running along the hem and the glass-facing side. Rooms kept curtained all day, like bedrooms or rental condos, get them especially fast. Beyond looking unpleasant, the mould spores and dust on the curtains scatter into the air every time you draw them open or shut — becoming an allergy trigger many homes can''t find the source of, even though it''s hanging right in front of them. For bedrooms, we recommend handling it alongside [mattress cleaning for dust-mite removal](/en/services/mattress-cleaning-phuket/) in one visit, since these two are the room''s main dust sources.

## Sheer versus blackout — different fabrics, different care

Sheer curtains are light and delicate, and too much heat or pull will leave them sagging out of shape. So the team uses a mild heat level, keeps distance from the fabric, and focuses on lifting dust and restoring their clear whiteness. Blackout and light-blocking curtains are thick and often have a coated backing, so we keep heat away from the coating directly but can work dust, smells and mould spots more intensively. Before starting, the team checks the fabric type of every curtain set and picks the right method for each one — not a single method across the whole house.
