## Move-in cleaning in Phuket — start in a genuinely clean home from day one

Moving into a new home is exciting, but if it is a resale home or a rental the last tenant just moved out of, what you take on is often not only the walls and floors but also the **grime and germs the previous occupant left behind** in the spots you cannot see — hand marks on switches and door handles, residue inside the cabinets and drawers that used to hold their things, grease built up in the kitchen, and germs in a bathroom shared for years. **Move-in cleaning in Phuket** clears all of this before you unpack, so your family can start in a home that is genuinely clean.

This service is especially suited to **homes with young children or older relatives**, whose immunity is more sensitive to germs and dust. Washing and sanitising inside the cabinets, in the sink and every touch point — before the children''s toys and your belongings go in — reduces the risk from the start.

## Best done while the home is empty

The key to this job is **timing**: it should be done after the last occupant leaves and before you move furniture in. An empty home lets the team reach every corner — opening and washing inside every cabinet, scrubbing the whole floor with nothing in the way, dusting the ceiling and high corners with ease. Once the whole home is washed and sanitised, you move your things in, and it is ready to live in from the very first night — no tidying up afterwards amid a pile of moving boxes.

If the home comes with the previous owner''s furniture, such as a sofa or mattress handed over, many customers add [steam sofa and mattress cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/) into the same visit, to sanitise and clear dust mites from the second-hand furniture at the same time.
