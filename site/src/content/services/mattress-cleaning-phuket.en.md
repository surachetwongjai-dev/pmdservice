## Dust mites love Phuket''s climate — and your mattress is their favourite home

Dust mites thrive best in warm, humid conditions, which matches Phuket''s air almost all year round. Your mattress is the ideal spot for them in the house: warmth from your body every night, moisture from sweat, and food from the skin flakes you shed while you sleep. The real problem isn''t just the mites themselves but the **mite waste**, an allergen that lifts into the air every time you turn over. Common symptoms are children waking with a run of sneezes, a blocked nose only in the morning, itchy rashes, or older adults whose asthma flares at night even though the day was fine.

The bad news is that sun-drying or vacuuming yourself only handles the surface, because dust mites are buried deep in the inner layer where sunlight and a home vacuum can''t reach. Our **mattress cleaning in Phuket** works directly on the inner layer of the mattress, killing the mites, pulling out the mite waste and built-up dust, and handling sweat marks and musty odours in one go.
