## Sofa cleaning in Phuket with steam — cleaner than it looks

Your sofa is the hardest-working furniture in the house: sitting, lounging, snacking and a favourite of pets. Dust, sweat and food crumbs build up in the fabric every day, becoming a feast for **dust mites** — a leading cause of allergies, morning sneezing, a blocked nose and rashes in children. Surface vacuuming alone cannot remove mites, because they live deep inside the cushions.

Our **sofa cleaning in Phuket** uses a three-step process that reaches the source: first a stain solution matched to the fabric, then **hot-steam sanitising (Steam Sanitise)** that penetrates deep into the fibres, dealing with bacteria, mould and mites, and finally a **German-standard extraction machine** that draws out the water and all the dirt with it. Many customers ask to see the waste water afterwards — and only then realise how dirty a "clean-looking" sofa really was.
