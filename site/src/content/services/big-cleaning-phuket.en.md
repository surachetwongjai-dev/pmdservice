## Big Cleaning in Phuket — one big clean done, for the occasions a home has to be spotless

There are occasions when a home has to be far cleaner than usual, and chipping away at it day by day will not do it in time — before welcoming relatives over New Year, before a house-blessing merit ceremony, before the photographer arrives to shoot a sale or rental listing, or simply when the home has built up grime for too long to keep up with regular cleaning. This is the moment for **Big Cleaning in Phuket**: a package that brings in a full team of maids together, splits the work by zone, and closes out a big clean of the whole home within a single day.

Unlike hiring a single [hourly maid](/en/services/maid-hourly-phuket/), which suits routine upkeep, Big Cleaning is designed for a one-off heavy job that needs both the numbers and specialist equipment. Prices start at ฿4,000, charged as a flat package by area, agreed clearly before we start — no add-ons on the day.

## A list that goes deeper than an ordinary tidy

The team works to a standard list agreed before we start, from ceiling to floor: remove cobwebs and dust on tall cabinets, wipe ceiling fans and light fittings, scrub limescale and soap scum across the whole bathroom, strip kitchen grease, clean door and window tracks and fly screens, right down to vacuuming, mopping and sanitising the floor under every movable piece of furniture. These are the spots regular weekly cleaning barely ever gets to — and they are what tell you whether a home is **genuinely clean** or just **looks clean**.

Many families pair Big Cleaning with another service in the same visit for better value — adding [steam sofa and mattress cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/) at the same time, for example, or polishing the hard floors throughout the home. Booked together, the package rate is better value than separate visits.
