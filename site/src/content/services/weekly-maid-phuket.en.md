## Weekly maid in Phuket — take your day off back from the housework

For families where both parents work full-time, the weekend often disappears into chasing down and wiping away everything that has built up over the week — time that should belong to the children and to rest. A **weekly maid in Phuket** solves this with a regular schedule of 1–2 visits a week, on the same day and time you choose. The maid keeps up the floors, bathrooms, kitchen and other routine jobs consistently, so the home never gets the chance to let grime build until it needs a big clear-out later.

The frequency most customers choose is once a week for condos and medium-sized homes, and twice a week for homes with young children or pets. The rate is based on the ฿400/hour maid rate (excl. tax), and anyone who locks in a long-term regular schedule gets a package rate that is better value than booking one-off. Ask for a quotation on LINE or call +66 61 965 6292 every day 08:00–18:00.

## Why "the same person" matters, and how we manage it

The heart of weekly work is not just cleanliness but familiarity, so we aim to assign the same maid to the same home every week, as far as the schedule allows. A maid coming to your home for the tenth time will know for herself which product must never touch the living-room wood floor, which vase must not be moved, and the corner you want given special attention. The first visit is like gathering information: the team notes the details of your home into a standing checklist, so that even in a week when a replacement is needed, the work still comes out to the same standard.

Many weekly customers are not home when the maid comes in. We support leaving a key with building management, using a keypad box, and arranging key hand-over and return by whatever system is agreed. It all rests on the same foundation as every service of ours: every maid is trained and police background-checked, and covered by ฿100,000 damage insurance on every visit, so you can trust us to look after the home even when no one is there.

## Start with the first visit, then decide on a fixed schedule

You do not have to commit from day one. Most customers start with a single [hourly maid](/en/services/maid-hourly-phuket/) booking to see the standard of work first, then lock in a regular day once they are happy. The team will help assess how many visits a week and how many hours each your home needs, based on its size, the number of people and any pets. For a home or office that needs a maid nearly every working day, a [monthly contract](/en/services/monthly-maid-phuket/) is steadier and easier to budget — the team is glad to compare the options with you before you decide.
