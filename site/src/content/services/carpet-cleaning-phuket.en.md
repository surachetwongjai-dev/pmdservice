## A carpet is a dust filter that never gets washed

Carpet fibres act like a horizontal air filter: dust, skin flakes, pet hair and grains of sand off the soles of shoes settle down at the **base of the fibres** every day. An ordinary vacuum picks up only the top layer, and in Phuket''s hot, humid air the moisture trapped in the carpet ferments into that distinctive **musty smell**. Homeowners often don''t notice because their nose has grown used to it — but a guest who has just walked in catches it from the first step.

Office carpet has it worse: the main walkways between desks slowly turn into dark grey lanes from dozens of pairs of shoes a day, and coffee drips build up under the meeting table. Pretty home rugs, meanwhile, face tangled pile, deeply embedded dust and marks from pets. All of this is why **carpet cleaning in Phuket** has to do more than scrub the surface.

## Water-extraction system — adjusted to each carpet''s fibres

We clean carpets with a water-extraction machine, the same system used on our [sofa cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/) page. The principle is to inject water and solution down to the fibre base and immediately draw it back up with the dust and dissolved dirt. What makes carpet a specialist job is **adjusting the machine to the fibre**: short-pile office carpet takes a wide head that presses long, even lanes quickly, while long-pile or Persian rugs need reduced pressure and several gentle repeat passes so the pile doesn''t tangle and lose its shape. Specific marks such as coffee, wine or ink are always treated with a targeted stain solution before we clean the whole carpet.

## Plan the drying time before you book

Carpet holds more water than a sofa cushion, so the question "when can we walk on it?" matters. The team air-blows to speed drying on every job and suggests running the air-con or a fan to clear the humidity. For offices, the formula our regulars use is to have the team clean in the evening after staff leave, so the carpet has all night to dry and everyone comes in the next morning as if nothing happened.
