## Post-construction cleaning in Phuket — the last job before handover

A newly finished home or condo isn''t ready to hand over the moment the builders put down their tools, because it''s still full of the traces of construction: debris, cement dust clinging to every surface, hardened cement on sanitaryware and tiles, and stickers and protective film still stuck to frames, glass and appliances. **Post-construction cleaning in Phuket** is the final detailing job that turns a building from "finished" into "ready to move in" before it reaches the end customer.

The main clients for this service are contractors, project owners and developers — especially housing estates, condos and rental pool villas in developing zones like [Mai Khao](/en/locations/maikhao/) and [Nai Yang](/en/locations/naiyang/) near the airport, where many units are handed over at once. We take work in batches and offer special rates for multi-unit jobs — the more units, the better the per-unit price.

## Systematic work, inspected before handover

Handover has standards to meet, so the team works systematically and inspects. It starts with collecting and removing debris, peeling off all stickers and protective film, clearing cement and grout off sanitaryware and tiles, lifting fine cement dust with repeated wet-wiping, wiping glass and sliding panels haze-free, and washing the new bathrooms and kitchen ready for real use — then finishing with an **inspection alongside the site supervisor**, unit by unit, against the agreed standard, so each unit is in a condition that can be handed to the customer straight away with no call-backs.
