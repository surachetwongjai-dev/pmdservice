## Pool villa cleaning in Phuket — finish before the next guest checks in

The rental pool villa business competes on time. The old group checks out late morning, the new group checks in that afternoon, and in that gap of just a few hours the whole house has to be reset as if no one had ever stayed. Phuket Maid Service's **pool villa cleaning in Phuket** is designed specifically for turnovers. Because guests paying pool-villa rates expect hotel-level cleanliness from the first step through the door.

## The poolside deck — the first thing guests walk on, and the riskiest spot in the house

What sets a pool villa apart from an ordinary rental is the poolside area. The deck takes strong sun, pool water and Phuket rain in turn all year round, so algae and slippery grime form quickly, especially in the rainy season. This is not just a matter of looks — it is the risk of a guest slipping by the pool, which can become a serious problem for both the guest and the villa's reviews. The team can sweep leaves, hose down the floor and wipe outdoor furniture as part of the round, and for sand-wash floors where the grime is deep-set or the surface too dull for ordinary washing to recover, we recommend the [sand-wash scrub and wax service](/en/services/sandwash-polishing-phuket/) at ฿100/sqm, charged by the actual area so the budget can be worked out in advance. The surface comes back to a firm, safe grip and a noticeably brighter colour.
