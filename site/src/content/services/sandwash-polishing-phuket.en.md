## Moss on a sandwash floor — a safety issue, not just looks

Sandwash is the go-to floor for outdoor spaces in the tropics — poolsides, terraces, garden paths and car parks — because the rough pebble surface resists slipping and stands up to sun and rain. But that same rough surface is a perfect foothold for **moss and mould**. Once Phuket''s rainy season arrives, a poolside that goes wet and dry all day develops a thin film of moss the eye can barely see, yet slippery enough to genuinely put someone on the ground. The most at-risk are children running around the pool and older adults. In a rental villa especially, one guest slipping and falling is a problem many times bigger than a year''s worth of floor care.

Scrubbing it yourself with a brush only reaches the top layer, because the moss roots down in the crevices between the pebbles where brush bristles can''t get. Within a few weeks the green marks are back in the same place.

## High-pressure washing + wax coating — exterior floors need both steps

First the team uses a **high-pressure washer** to clean lane by lane, zone by zone. The force of the water reaches every crevice between the pebbles, driving out moss, dirt marks and standing-water stains from the deep grooves that hand-scrubbing can''t touch (see the process in action in the video above). The second step, which many companies skip, is finishing with a **wax coating**. The wax layer blocks new grime from setting in fast, makes the pebble colour look fresher, dries water faster after rain, and makes the next cleaning round far easier.
