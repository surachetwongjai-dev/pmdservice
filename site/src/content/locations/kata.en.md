## Before the tourist season arrives, properties in Kata need to be ready

Every year, as the rainy season turns to the cool season, jobs from the Kata side always come in the same direction: owners of small hotels and guesthouses want a whole-building [Deep Cleaning](/en/services/deep-cleaning-phuket/) before taking a new round of guests. Rooms that stayed curtained through the low season build up dust, damp and a stale smell, so one big clean before the season is an investment property owners here make as a matter of routine. Phuket Maid Service assesses the site first, then quotes based on the real condition. Big Cleaning starts at ฿4,000.

## A family beach means more varied properties than you would expect

Kata is one of the beaches most chosen by travelling families in Phuket, so the accommodation here ranges from guesthouses tucked in the sois, to small family-run hotels, to sea-view condos on the Kata Noi hillside. Each type needs a different kind of work. Small hotels often call in a [hotel cleaning team](/en/services/hotel-cleaning-phuket/) for extra support when guests are full, while condo owners tend to book an hourly maid at ฿400/hr to reset the room before they fly in to stay themselves.

## Mattresses and curtains are the hidden work a seaside area cannot ignore

Sea humidity combined with guests turning over all year means mattresses in Kata properties take far more of a beating than a normal home. Sweat stains, dust mites and built-up odours are things fresh linens cannot fully hide, so many owners book [mattress cleaning](/en/services/mattress-cleaning-phuket/) alongside the regular room reset, plus curtain washing, which the salt air dulls faster than usual. Both can be quoted by size and quantity on LINE.

## Weekly rentals mean the room must be spotless every round

What sets Kata apart from Patong is that guests stay longer. Many properties rent by the week to families on holiday, so turnovers are less frequent but more detailed, because the new guests will treat that room as home for the whole week. Our team works to the owner's checklist — from the kitchen guests actually cook in, to the balcony hit by sea spray, to the washing machine and shelves a long-stay guest uses every day — then sends photo reports to owners who are not on-site.
