## Kamala — the beach for people who "live" here, not just visit

Between the buzz of Patong and the luxury of Surin, Kamala is the beach quiet enough for people to decide to settle in for the long haul. Most residents here are retired Europeans and families renting a beachfront holiday condo or a hillside villa for several months at a time. Their needs are different from a tourist's — not one big clean and done, but a maid who comes regularly, the same person, at the same time, who understands what they ask for.

The Phuket Maid Service team speaks both Thai and English, and every member is trained and police background-checked before starting work, with ฿100,000 damage insurance on every job. It is exactly this kind of trust that leads long-stay residents in Kamala to open their doors to us year after year.

## Regular weekly work is the heart of this area

The package Kamala customers choose most is a [weekly maid](/en/services/weekly-maid-phuket/), once or twice a week, a few hours each time, at ฿400 per hour. The team brings a full kit of equipment and cleaning products — the owner needs to prepare nothing at all.

The add-on most often booked alongside it is [steam sofa and mattress cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/), because living in a home for many hours a day, plus the sea humidity, means fabric upholstery collects dust mites faster than a room that is shut up and empty. Families with young children or elderly members prone to allergies often set a cleaning cycle well in advance. And for the big picture windows that frame the sea view — the selling point of condos in this area — we offer [glass cleaning](/en/services/window-cleaning-phuket/) that lifts the salt haze until it looks like there is no pane there at all.

## Across the island, but always plannable in advance

Kamala is on the west coast of the island. Our team travels from our office in Pa Khlok, Thalang, taking about 35–40 minutes. For regular work we therefore recommend booking at least one day ahead, to lock in the exact slot you want every week — especially during high season, when queues along the beach areas are particularly full.

A good number of Kamala properties sit on narrow, steep hill roads. Our team knows these routes well and travels by car with a full kit. Just tell us the location and parking spot when you book.
