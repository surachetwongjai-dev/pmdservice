## Many Mai Khao villa owners are not on the island — and that is exactly what we are good at

Mai Khao is the longest and quietest beach in the north of Phuket, lined with luxury beachfront resorts alternating with pool-villa developments that owners have bought to rent out through Airbnb and other platforms, leaning on the strength of being close to the airport. Many owners are in Bangkok or overseas. What they need is not just a maid, but a team they can genuinely brief from afar, book online, see clear appointments for, and receive photos from after every job to confirm the villa is ready for the next guest. Phuket Maid Service is the first in Phuket to offer online maid booking, and we have looked after villas like these in Mai Khao continuously.

## The rental-villa formula: guest turnovers + scheduled Big Cleaning

The pattern most Mai Khao villa owners use is an [Airbnb clean](/en/services/airbnb-cleaning-phuket/) every time a guest checks out — clearing the kitchen, making the beds, wiping marks and vacuuming every room — alternating with a [Big Cleaning](/en/services/big-cleaning-phuket/) for the whole house every couple of months or before high season, which starts at ฿4,000 by villa size, to deal with the spots a normal turnover does not reach: the ceiling, under the furniture, and the mould that comes with the rainy season.

Villas with a private pool often add [pool villa cleaning](/en/services/pool-villa-cleaning-phuket/) to cover the poolside area and outdoor furniture in the same visit. For beachfront resorts, we take on maid-team reinforcement during busy guest periods and targeted deep-cleaning work, priced as a flat rate by the scale of the job — ask for a quote.

## The distance that becomes an advantage

Many people assume Mai Khao is at the far end of the island, so any team you call will be slow. With us it is the opposite. The Phuket Maid Service office sits in Pa Khlok, Thalang, on the same northern side of the island, and the team drives to Mai Khao in only about 25–30 minutes. Scheduling is therefore far more flexible than calling a team from the south of the island, and we can often slot in a job before a guest checks in.

Every member of staff is trained and police background-checked, every job is covered by ฿100,000 damage insurance, and the team comes fully equipped with supplies and products, speaking both Thai and English.
