## Real homes, real families — the challenge of maid work in Chalong

If Patong is the tourists' district, Chalong is the district of people who wake up to go to work and come home to dinner. Gated estates have sprung up around Chalong Circle for years — detached houses, townhomes, and rental homes of foreigners running boat and diving businesses around Chalong Bay. Homes with people living in them every day generate housework that never ends, which is why our [house cleaning service](/en/services/house-cleaning-phuket/) has a particularly dense base of regular customers in Chalong.

## A hard-working Thai kitchen is where a maid has to be genuinely good

Most homes in the Chalong area cook every day. Grease on the wall behind the stove, the extractor-hood filter and a sink used morning and night are the real thing, quite unlike a condo kitchen that is barely ever switched on. Our team carries a degreaser separate from general cleaner and knows what can and cannot be used on each type of stone counter. Work like this can be booked as an [hourly maid](/en/services/maid-hourly-phuket/) at ฿400/hr on a regular weekly cycle, or you can start with a single visit to try us first.

## Big Cleaning — the whole home's big job of the year

The moments when Chalong people call for a Big Cleaning are usually clear: before Songkran or New Year when relatives gather, after an extension is finished, or on taking handover of a new home. Our [Big Cleaning](/en/services/big-cleaning-phuket/) works through the whole house, from the ceiling, ceiling fans, furniture gaps and window tracks to cement dust and paint marks left after the builders. It starts at ฿4,000, priced against the actual size of the home before we begin. Homes just handed over should look at post-renovation cleaning alongside it.

## Getting into gated estates without a hitch

Almost every estate in Chalong has a guardhouse and a visitor-pass system. Our team brings ID cards ready to swap every time, and if the estate requires the homeowner to register names in advance, we send the team list before the job day. Many Chalong owners are out of the house during the day, so they like to leave the key with a neighbour or use a code lockbox, then wait for before-and-after photos on LINE.
