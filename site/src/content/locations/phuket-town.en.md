## Cleaning work in a town that is alive all day

Phuket Town is not a holiday district — it is the real economic heart of the island. Sino-Portuguese shophouses in the Old Town run as shops and cafés, office buildings line the main roads, several schools sit in the town centre, and homes are tucked into every lane. Our customers here are not tourists but business owners and families who want a cleaning team that understands the rhythm of the business day.

## Working when there are no customers is the single most important condition

Shops and cafés cannot do a big clean while they are open. Our [office and shop cleaning service](/en/services/office-cleaning-phuket/) therefore schedules mainly around the business's own hours. Shops that open late book the team for 08:00 before opening; offices book the evening near 18:00 after staff finish. Regular weekly or monthly office work is priced as a flat rate by area and frequency — ask for a quote on LINE. Occasional one-off jobs are charged as an hourly maid at ฿400/hr.

## In the rainy season, moss out front is a bigger deal than you think

Phuket's eight months of rain make the pavement and the ground in front of shophouses grow slippery moss every year. For a shop with customers walking in and out all day, a slippery shopfront is a risk the owner cannot leave alone. [Sandwash flooring](/en/services/sandwash-polishing-phuket/) — a popular material on older town buildings — we scrub clean of moss and black stains at ฿100/sqm. For buildings with marble entrance lobbies, we offer [marble polishing](/en/services/marble-polishing-phuket/) at ฿250/sqm, bringing back the shine the floor had the day it was laid.

## Shopfront glass and signage are the face of the business

Old Town shophouses compete on their frontage. Big glass panes clouded by road dust and rain spray make a shop look older than it is. Our team handles [glass cleaning](/en/services/window-cleaning-phuket/) for both ground-floor shopfront panes and office-building glass, finishing the frames and signage in the same round. Many shops book this on a monthly cycle alongside cleaning the shop floor.

## The area we reach fastest

From our office in Pa Khlok, Thalang, reaching central Phuket Town takes only 30–35 minutes — the shortest of all the main areas we serve. Urgent jobs like a tidy-up before opening or after an event can therefore be scheduled more flexibly than for the beach zones. The team works every day 08:00–18:00 and speaks both Thai and English.
