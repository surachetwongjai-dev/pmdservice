## Cherng Talay — the densest pool-villa sub-district in Phuket

If you count pool villas per square kilometre, no area on the island beats Cherng Talay — from world-class projects in the Laguna zone, to holiday homes along Si Sunthon Road, to luxury estates hidden all along Soi Pasak. Most properties here do not have the owner living in them year-round; they are in the hands of villa managers and agencies looking after many at once. Their challenge is not simply "finding a maid" but finding a team that holds the same standard at every villa, every time, and can be trusted to hold the keys to a home worth tens of millions.

Phuket Maid Service answers this with a system different from hiring individual maids. Villa managers can schedule a whole month of cleaning in advance and let our team run it without chasing them villa by villa.
