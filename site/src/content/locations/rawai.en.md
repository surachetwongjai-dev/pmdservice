## Rawai — the area for people who live in Phuket rather than visit it

Unlike the west-coast beaches that revolve around daily tourists, Rawai is where expats choose to actually live. Rental homes and villas are hidden along the sois between Rawai and Nai Harn beach, and many residents sign leases by the year. What this area needs is not a one-off room reset but a maid who comes to look after the home consistently, almost as part of daily life — which is exactly what the [weekly maid](/en/services/weekly-maid-phuket/) and [monthly maid](/en/services/monthly-maid-phuket/) services that people in Rawai book most are for.

## Regular customers are the heart of the work here

The pattern Rawai customers prefer is to set fixed days — say every Tuesday and Friday — so the maid comes on schedule without having to rebook each time. Basic work is charged hourly at ฿400/hr, while weekly or monthly regular packages are priced as a flat rate based on the actual scope — just ask for a quote. Many homes let us look after the place continuously until the maid knows the house well enough that the owner simply leaves the door open and heads out to run errands. Trust at this level is possible because every maid is police background-checked and every job is backed by ฿100,000 damage insurance.

## English communication, from the quote to the job itself

What long-stay customers worry about most is not the price but explaining what they want and having no one understand. That is why our coordination team handles everything fully in English — interpreting a home's specific task list, restrictions around pets, or products someone is allergic to. Customers can write their instructions in English on LINE, and the team will confirm the work back to make sure it matches before entering the home every time.

## Soft furnishings used every day need more than a dusting

A home lived in all year is different from a rental room in that the sofa, rugs and mattress are used hard every day. In Rawai's humid air, soft furnishings build up dust mites, pet hair and a stale smell faster than owners realise. Steam-based [sofa cleaning](/en/services/sofa-cleaning-phuket/) is therefore a job regulars here call in periodically, usually scheduled alongside the regular cleaning round so it all finishes in one day. Pricing depends on the size and number of seats — just ask before booking.
