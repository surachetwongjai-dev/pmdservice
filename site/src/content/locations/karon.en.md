## The Karon beach road — hotels and resorts that must be spotless every day

Karon is among the longest beaches on the island, and the whole beach road is lined almost end to end with hotels and resorts. What they all share is that their cleanliness standard has to stay consistent every day, whether guests are full or not. Phuket Maid Service takes jobs in Karon both on ongoing contracts for [hotels](/en/services/hotel-cleaning-phuket/) and [resorts](/en/services/resort-cleaning-phuket/), and as one-off work for private room owners.

## High season fills the rooms — what to do when the regular housekeeping team is not enough

The problem hotel managers in Karon face every year is that during high season the rooms are full for weeks on end, but the regular housekeeping team stays the same size. The solution many choose is to bring in an outside backup team just for the peak. We can supply staff both by the day and on a continuous weekly or monthly basis. Every maid is trained to the company's hotel standards and police background-checked before starting, and hotel HR can obtain the full supporting documents. Pricing depends on the number of staff and the duration — just ask for a quote.

## The bathroom decides the reviews at a beachfront property

Properties around Karon beach have to cope with sand tracked in on guests' feet every day, sunscreen residue on the sanitary ware, and hard-water marks on the shower glass. The bathroom is therefore the spot our team is most reminded about when taking jobs here. The team separates cleaning products by surface type, scrubs the grout, clears the salt marks off the glass panels, and disinfects every touch point on the sanitary ware, so the bathroom stays in the same condition as the day it first opened.

## Holiday condos — looking after rooms for owners who are far away

The hillsides and back-beach sois of Karon are full of holiday condos owners bought to rent out, some visiting only a few times a year. The job we are assigned most often is opening the room to air it out, wiping down the dust, checking the room's condition while it is vacant, and a full [condo cleaning](/en/services/condo-cleaning-phuket/) before the owner flies in to stay or before a new tenant moves in. General work is charged as an hourly maid at ฿400/hr, while a room left closed for a long time is better suited to Big Cleaning from ฿4,000.
