## Taking handover of a new room in Bang Tao? Do not move in before clearing the construction dust

In the last few years the Bang Tao beach road has become one of the densest clusters of new condo projects in Phuket, with units handed over to Thai and foreign owners continuously — and almost everyone discovers the same truth: a "new" room does not mean a ready-to-live-in one. Fine construction dust coats every surface, paint marks and silicone bits stick along the glass edges, and debris sits in the tracks of sliding doors.

The job we are called into projects here most often is therefore [post-renovation and after-decoration cleaning](/en/services/post-renovation-cleaning-phuket/), along with a [Big Cleaning](/en/services/big-cleaning-phuket/) before move-in, which starts at ฿4,000 by unit size. The team arrives with a vacuum and products made specifically for construction residue, cleaning in detail from the ceiling down to the grout lines, so the room is genuinely ready for furniture to move in.

## From pool villas to shopfronts around Boat Avenue

Set back from the beach, Bang Tao is also full of rental pool villas and holiday homes. Owners often book a clean before guests arrive, paired with [marble polishing](/en/services/marble-polishing-phuket/) at ฿250/sqm, because the luxury units here are floored in marble almost throughout, and a gleaming floor is the first thing guests notice when they open the door.

The other group that sets Bang Tao apart from neighbouring sub-districts is business owners. The Boat Avenue roundabout and the nearby community malls are the liveliest cluster of restaurants, cafes and small offices on the west coast. Many use our team for a deep clean in the early morning before opening — the kitchen floor, the shopfront glass and the customer seating area — because we can start from 08:00 every day, including weekends. And homes with permanent residents can book an hourly maid at ฿400/hr, with a full kit of equipment, so there is nothing to prepare.
